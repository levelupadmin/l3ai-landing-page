The curriculum

Everything you'll learn, module by module.

Every module teaches a skill and ends in a build. By the last one, your product is live.

Module 01AI Fluency+

Get expert-level output from any AI, every time.

Most people get average answers because they ask average questions. This module turns AI into your sharpest work tool.

  1. How LLMs actually work: tokens, context, why they hallucinate
  2. The four prompt patterns that make output reliable
  3. System prompts, personas and structured output
  4. Your personal prompt library, reusable across every job
  5. Research grounded in your own documents, with sources
  6. Picking the right model: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, NotebookLM

You walk out witha prompt library and a research assistant built on your own documents.

Module 02AI Image & Design+

A full design team, inside your prompt box.

Professional visuals and a complete brand identity, generated on demand and consistent every single time.

  1. How image models work and how to direct them
  2. Prompt structure for images: styles, references, composition
  3. A consistent visual system, not one-off lucky images
  4. Logos, social visuals and hero images for a real brand
  5. Editing, templates and repeatable design workflows
  6. Tools: Midjourney, Ideogram, Canva AI

You walk out witha full brand and image kit, built by you.

Module 03AI Video, Voice & Content Engines+

A media team that never sleeps.

Produce video and voice that used to need a studio, then wire it into a content engine that publishes every week.

  1. Scripting and scene prompting for AI video
  2. Voice cloning and narration, done right
  3. Editing and assembling a finished piece
  4. One idea turned into a week of multi-format content
  5. Content calendars that run with AI assistance
  6. Tools: Runway, Google Veo, ElevenLabs, Descript

You walk out witha promo video with AI voiceover and a content engine producing a real week of output.

Module 04Automation+

Your busywork, on autopilot.

Map the work you repeat every week and turn it into automations that run without you touching them.

  1. The trigger-action model: the anatomy of every automation
  2. Mapping a real business process end to end
  3. Multi-step automations in Make and Zapier
  4. n8n and workflow orchestration
  5. APIs, webhooks and MCP, in plain terms and used for real
  6. Debugging and keeping automations alive

You walk out withlive automations running on your actual tools, connected through real APIs.

Module 05AI Agents+

Build your first AI employee.

Software that plans, acts, checks its work and finishes the job while you're doing something else.

  1. Agent anatomy: goal, tools, memory, loop
  2. When an agent beats an automation
  3. Giving agents real data, actions and a human in the loop
  4. Error handling and reliability testing
  5. Putting an agent on a real recurring job and measuring the hours it saves
  6. Tools: Relevance AI, n8n agents, custom GPTs

You walk out witha dependable agent doing a real job, with a measured result.

Module 06No-Code Products+

Your first real app, without writing code.

Turn everything you've built into something people can open, click and use.

  1. Product thinking: from problem to buildable idea
  2. Apps, sites and dashboards with no-code tools
  3. Data and authentication basics
  4. Your agent, wired inside your app
  5. Polish and usability that makes it feel real
  6. Tools: Lovable, Bubble, v0, Supabase

You walk out witha working app with your agent running inside it.

Module 07Your MVPFoundations finish line · Pro entry point+

Your idea, live, with real users.

The build month: one real product on your own data, launched and presented at Demo Day.

  1. Writing a product spec and scoping an MVP
  2. Vibe coding with Claude Code, Codex and Cursor
  3. Applied RAG: your product answering from your own data
  4. Connecting real data sources safely
  5. Positioning, landing pages and getting first users
  6. Telling the build story at a public Demo Day

You walk out witha launched MVP with real users, presented live.

Module 08Production Engineering+

Build the way professionals ship.

The depth every other program skips: real code, real retrieval, real orchestration.

  1. LLM APIs in code: Python, OpenAI and Anthropic SDKs, Git
  2. RAG at scale: embeddings, vector databases, hybrid search
  3. Multi-agent systems: orchestration, function calling, memory
  4. LangGraph and agent frameworks
  5. Governance, guardrails, safety and cost control

You walk out withyour product's core rebuilt production-grade.

Module 09Deploy & Scale+

Live on the internet. Proven with numbers.

Ship it to real users, watch it run, and pass a senior engineer's code review.

  1. Evaluation suites: “it seems fine” becomes numbers you can defend
  2. Deployment to live environments: Vercel, cloud, app stores
  3. Monitoring, latency and cost in production
  4. Scaling, hardening and optimization
  5. The senior code review, passed
  6. Final Demo Day: your deployed product, presented to the room

You walk out witha deployed, monitored product with a live URL.

Questions

The things people ask first.

I'm not technical. I've genuinely never built anything. +

That's exactly who Foundations and Mastery are for. They start at zero. If you can use ChatGPT, you can start.

Is this actually live, or a recording mill? +

100% live and weekend-led. Recordings exist only for catch-up. We'll never sell you a recording as “live.”

How much time does it take every week? +

Around 8 to 10 hours: two live weekend sessions, optional weekday doubt clinics, and your own build time.

Will this get me a job, a raise, a promotion? +

No promises, and we won't imply any. You leave with capability and a portfolio of real, shipped work. What you do with it is yours.

Isn't all of this free on YouTube? +

The information is. The structure isn't: a deadline, feedback on your actual build, and a room that expects your demo on Sunday. You're paying to actually build.

₹90,000 is a lot. What if I join and it's not for me? +

That's what the selection exists to prevent. We read your application and interview you before any tuition moves. If we think you won't get value, we say no and tell you why.

How do I know this works? +

Everything is checkable before you apply: the curriculum, the fee, the mentors. And the output is public. Every builder demos a working MVP and a deployed product in front of the room and invited guests.

Can I start with Foundations and continue later? +

Yes. Pro picks up exactly where Foundations stops. Bought separately the two cost ₹1,30,000; Mastery is the same journey for ₹90,000. The saving is for deciding once.

Can I pay in installments? +

Yes. No-cost EMI on every pathway, with the monthly amount, tenure and total shown together. Mastery also has an in-house two-part split.

Is the ₹700 application fee really refundable? +

Yes. Returned if we say no, credited to your tuition if you're in. It exists to filter out the casual, not to make money.
